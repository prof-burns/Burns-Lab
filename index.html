The Burns Lab

We study the microbes that live in and on the human body and how they relate to cancer and other diseases. We also run programs on AI, character education, and science communication.

Our work Join the lab

Our Work

Microbiome and Disease

How the microbes in and on us change with skin, breast, and colorectal cancers and other diseases.

AI and Human Flourishing

Directing Loyola's AIHF minor and leading faculty workshops on generative AI in STEM teaching.

Character Education

Co-leading a three-year Educating Character Initiative grant at Loyola.

Program Assessment

Assessment Faculty Fellow in the Provost's Office.

Teaching

Cell biology, metagenomics, global biological risks, and courses co-taught with philosophy.

Science Communication

Public talks on science, ethics, and religion, and outreach with community partners.

        Join the Lab

        We're always looking for motivated postdocs, staff scientists, graduate students, and undergraduates. Some experience with R, Python, or the shell, and with statistics, helps. Send a CV and a short cover letter to mburns16@luc.edu. Loyola undergraduates should also apply through the Biology Undergraduate Research Portal.
